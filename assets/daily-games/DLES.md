# DLES a.k.a Daily Logic Exercises

> An open mirror of the **1058** daily games collected at [dles.gg](https://dles.gg/games/newest?ref=assets.dles.gg) ordered by newest first. Made a daily game yourself? [Submit it to the collection!](https://dles.gg/submit-game?ref=assets.dles.gg).

- [Who Farted?](https://whofartedgame.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Everyone stuck in an elevator makes 1 statement, and the culprit who farted always lies. Find the person whose lie makes every other statement true, with 3 wrong accusations allowed and a hint that narrows down where the culprit is standing. The daily notice adds twists, such as a mime who only points or an innocent who lies too, and difficulty rises from Monday to Sunday.
- [Sonar Puzzle](https://sonarpuzzle.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Find 5 hidden subs on a 6x6 grid with 10 pings. The subs always sit in the same chain shape, in 1 of 4 orientations, and each miss shows how many steps away the nearest unfound sub is, with diagonal steps counting as 1. Sink all 5 before your pings run out.
- [Word Spill](https://playwordspill.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Clear a 5x5 letter grid, 6x6 on Sundays, by tracing words of 3 or more touching letters in any direction. Used tiles vanish, the letters above fall and empty columns slide left, with no new letters added. Tiles carry point values that longer words multiply, and a word of 5 or more letters charges the tiles around it to score double in your next word. 3 swaps of side-by-side tiles help when the board stalls, and unused ones earn bonus points.
- [EraGuessr](https://eraguessr.ai/play/daily?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Look around 5 AI-generated 360° scenes of historical moments, then pin where each one is on a world map and set its year on a timeline. Location and year are each worth up to 5,000 points per scene, scored on how close you land, with 90 seconds per scene.
- [Pipcross](https://puzzmallow.com/pipcross?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Place every die from the pool into the grid so its pips match the clues beside each row and column. Each clue lists the runs of consecutive pips in that line, in order, and a run can carry across neighboring dice. 3 boards a day: Mini, Classic and Spin, where the 2, 3 and 6 dice can also be rotated to face the right way.
- [Spectrle](https://spectrle.games/?utm_source=dlesgg&utm_medium=post&ref=assets.dles.gg) - Name the movie from a disc built out of 500 frames sampled evenly across the whole film, with its release year and tagline as starting clues. You have 5 guesses, and each wrong guess or skip reveals a written hint and a clearer version of the disc. A second mode does the same for video games.
- [Scorewit Footyphoria](https://www.scorewit.com/footyphoria?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 6 multiple-choice questions on Premier League history since 1992-93, 4 options each and 100 points per question. Facts are computed from match data and cover title winners, clubs and results season by season. Everyone gets the same 6, with a practice mode and a club filter.
- [Matchsticks](https://thehardest.us/matchsticks?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Find the hidden 5-letter word. Each guess shows only how many of its letters also appear in the answer, never which ones or where they sit. Rule letters in or out on the deduction ledger and solve it in as few guesses as you can.
- [Kpop Heardle](https://kpopheardle.org?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Listen to a clip of up to 30 seconds from each of 3 K-pop songs and pick the right track from 4 options. You get 1 pick per song, and the clip can be replayed as often as you like before you answer. Your score is how many of the 3 you name.
- [Guess the News](https://guessthenews.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - A real news headline is shown with 1 word blanked out. Type a guess for the missing word, and each one is rated from cold to hot by how close its meaning is to the answer. Keep narrowing in until you find it, with an optional hint or a reveal if you get stuck.
- [Periodle](https://heptaveegesimal.com/periodle?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Guess a word spelled from 5 elements of the periodic table in 8 tries, so guesses run from 5 to 10 letters. - [Guess Mint](https://guessmint.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Work out who issued a real historical coin and roughly when it was struck. You learn how far your issuer's capital is from the right one and which way to go, and whether the coin is earlier or later than your year. Get both right within 6 guesses.
