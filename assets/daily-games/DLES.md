# DLES a.k.a Daily Logic Exercises

> An open mirror of the **972** daily games collected at [dles.gg](https://dles.gg/games/newest?ref=assets.dles.gg) ordered by newest first. Made a daily game yourself? [Submit it to the collection!](https://dles.gg/submit-game?ref=assets.dles.gg).

- [GnarlyQ](https://gnarlyq.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 5 trivia questions, each showing 10 possible answers of which exactly 5 are correct. Tap the correct ones before the clock runs out. Each correct pick is worth more than the last, 2 up to 6, so finding all 5 in a question scores 20 and a flawless run scores 100. A wrong tap ends the question and wipes its points, unless one of your 2 shields blocks it. Lock in at any time to bank what you have earned.
- [Scorewit Super Over](https://www.scorewit.com/superover?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 6 multiple-choice questions on the Indian Premier League since 2008, 4 options each and 100 points per question. Facts come from ball-by-ball data and cover run chases, super overs and season results. Everyone gets the same 6, with a practice mode, a team filter and a browsable season archive.
- [Scorewit Gridiron](https://www.scorewit.com/gridiron?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 6 multiple-choice questions on NFL seasons from 1999 onward, 4 options each and 100 points per question. Facts are computed from play-by-play data and cover Super Bowl winners, upsets and season results. Everyone gets the same 6, with a practice mode, a team filter and a records archive.
- [Scorewit Baseball](https://www.scorewit.com/baseball?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 6 multiple-choice questions on World Series history back to 1903, 4 options each and 100 points per question. Facts come from historical game records and cover pennants, game results and series outcomes. Everyone gets the same 6, with a practice mode, a team filter and a browsable records archive.
- [Scorewit F1](https://www.scorewit.com/f1?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 6 multiple-choice questions on Formula 1 history back to 1950, 4 options each and 100 points per question. Facts are computed from race records and cover champions, circuits and results season by season. Everyone gets the same 6, plus a practice mode, a driver and constructor filter, and a countdown to the next grand prix.
- [Scorewit Cricket](https://www.scorewit.com/cricket?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 6 multiple-choice questions on the mens ODI and T20 World Cups, 4 options each and 100 points per question. Facts are drawn from ball-by-ball match data and cover finals, champions, chases and collapses. Everyone gets the same 6, with a practice mode, a team filter and a browsable tournament archive.
- [Majority Game](https://www.majoritygame.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 5 prompts, each asking what comes to mind first. Type not your own answer but the one you think most other players typed, then see what percentage of the crowd matched it. Points come from landing on the majority, so the popular answer beats the clever one.
- [Scorewit — Daily World Cup Trivia](https://www.scorewit.com/worldcup?ref=assets.dles.gg) - 6 multiple-choice questions on World Cup history from 1930 onward, 4 options each and 100 points on the line per question. Facts are computed from official match data and cover champions, finals and results. Everyone faces the same 6, with a practice mode and per-team archives alongside.
- [Concentration](https://concentration.fun?ref=assets.dles.gg) - A category lands on screen, anything from Sandwiches to Pixar films, and you type as many members of it as you can before a 2 minute clock runs out. Each correct answer scores by how rare it is, so obscure picks pay more than obvious ones. A hint button and voice input are there if you stall, and everyone gets the same category so scores compare directly.
- [Puzzmo - Ribbit](https://www.puzzmo.com/play/ribbit?ref=assets.dles.gg) - A 4x4 board of letters wired together by lines. Trace a path along those links, by dragging, tapping or typing, to spell a word of 4 or more letters, then submit it. Each word found strips out the connections and letters it consumed, shrinking the board and making the remaining finds easier to spot. The longest word carries a star, and the score drops the longer you take or the more words you leave hidden.
- [Giffle - Friends](https://giffle.com/friends?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Identify the Friends episode from up to 6 GIFs, revealed one at a time, within 6 guesses. Search by episode title or season to lock in a pick. A yellow marker means the right season but the wrong episode, red means the wrong season, and an arrow shows whether the answer falls earlier or later in the run.
- [Amountle](https://amountle.dev?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Objects tumble into a clear container while you watch, then a 15 second clock starts and you dial in how many landed inside. 5 rounds, each with a different object and vessel, and the closer the estimate the better the score. The container can be dragged to spin for a second look before submitting.
- [putt.day](https://putt.day?ref=assets.dles.gg) - A single hole of 3D mini golf, the same one for everyone. Drag back from the ball and release to putt, pulling further for more power, and drag elsewhere to swing the camera around the course. Sink it in as few strokes as you can. Water sends the ball back to where it was, though a first shot into water is forgiven. An archive holds the earlier holes, and players can build holes of their own.
- [TubeCount](https://tubecount.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Estimate how many views a real YouTube video has, working from its thumbnail and upload year alone. You get 3 guesses worth 50%, 30% and 20% of the score, so the cold read matters most. Guess 2 reveals the title and guess 3 the channel and subscriber count. Each answer is stamped green within 2x, yellow within 5x and red beyond that.
- [Wild Lies](https://paskozhelev.github.io/manipdigital-games-app/wild-lies?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Animals sit around a table, each making 1 statement about who is guilty, and a counter tells you how many culprits and how many liars are in the case. Tag each animal as culprit, liar, both or neither. Guilt and honesty are separate, so a culprit may tell the truth and an innocent may lie, and only 1 arrangement fits every statement.
- [The Magical Ferry](https://magicalferry.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - A themed river-crossing puzzle. Ferry a cast of characters from one bank to the other within a fixed number of rides and a fixed boat capacity, knowing that certain pairs cannot be left alone together on either shore. Difficulty climbs through the week, from Easy on Monday to Extra Hard on Sunday.
- [Zonday](https://zonday.app?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Guess what a real Amazon US product was selling for, starting from just its photo, star rating and review count. Each guess returns an arrow plus a temperature reading, and more product detail unlocks as you go. Landing within 1% counts as correct, or within $3 on items under $300.
- [Tile Knight](https://tileknight.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Clear every tile on a 6x6 board. Tapping a tile removes it and dictates your next move, where a number sends you that many squares in any direction and a chess piece moves the way that piece does, so a knight jumps in an L while a bishop, rook or queen slides to the first tile it meets. Strand yourself with no legal move and the path has failed. Easy, medium and hard boards each day.
- [Neon Protocol](https://paskozhelev.github.io/manipdigital-games-app/neon-protocol?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Work out a hidden code of 3 to 5 digits, each between 1 and 5, from clues about comparisons, sums, multiples and the spread between the largest and smallest digit. Cross out impossible values in the elimination grid until 1 code is left. In glitched rounds exactly 1 clue is a lie and spotting which is part of the job.
- [Symbiomes](https://paskozhelev.github.io/manipdigital-games-app/symbiomes?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Drag each animal onto a 3x3 map of grass, water, sand and mud so every clue holds at once. Clues fix an animal to a terrain, sit it next to another animal or an empty tile, count the animals in a column, or set the exact number of steps between 2 animals. Only tiles sharing an edge count as adjacent, diagonals do not.
- [Logic Gems](https://paskozhelev.github.io/manipdigital-games-app/logic-gems?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Fill a 3x3 board with 9 gems, one for every pairing of 3 colors and 3 shapes. Clue tiles show small arrangements exactly as they sit on the board, never rotated or mirrored, and may pin down only a color, only a shape, or both. A clue on a red background means that exact pattern appears nowhere on the board.
- [Packle](https://packle.top?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Guess how many identical circles fit inside today's shape without overlapping. One circle sits in the outline for scale, and each of your 5 guesses comes back rated Ice Cold, Cold, Warm, Hot or Boiling depending on how far off your number is. Some shapes leave gaps where no further circle fits.
- [YGOdle](https://www.ygodle.net?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Name the hidden Yu-Gi-Oh! monster, spell or trap card with unlimited guesses. Each guess is scored column by column on type, race, level, ATK, DEF, attribute and release year, where green marks an exact match and an arrow says whether the real value is higher or lower. A 4th mode asks which of 2 monsters has the higher ATK.
- [Radiordle](https://www.radiordle.org?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Read a real medical scan (X-ray, CT, MRI or ultrasound) and name the diagnosis. You get 5 attempts, and every wrong answer reveals another clue about the case to narrow the field. An archive holds the earlier cases.
- [TypeCircuit](https://typecircuit.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Type out a circuit of 3 quotes pulled from video games, movies, and anime, going for speed and accuracy at once. Words per minute, elapsed time, typos, accuracy, and character count are tracked live, and everyone races the same 3 quotes. Streaks, replays of past circuits, and 4 colour themes are included.
- [Prompetitor](https://prompetitor.com?ref=assets.dles.gg) - You get 90 seconds to study a piece of artwork and describe it in a written prompt. An image model then generates a picture from your words, and you are scored on how closely it matches the original, with a small bonus for speed. Everyone works from the same artwork and results are ranked on a global board. You can play as a guest without an account.
- [majority.wtf](https://majority.wtf?ref=assets.dles.gg) - One question goes out to everyone each round, and the answers people give become the board for the next round. Your job is to guess the single most common answer from the previous round, and you get 5 tries. A guess turns yellow if it landed in the top 5 and orange if it reached the top 10, while grey means it missed the board entirely. After guessing you answer the new question, which feeds the following board.
- [Chrono NBA](https://chrono.quest/basketball.html?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Drag 5 moments from NBA history into chronological order. You get 3 tries to arrange the full sequence correctly.
- [DOGDLE](https://dogdle.org?ref=assets.dles.gg) - A real shelter dog waiting for adoption is shown in a set of photos, and you work out both breeds in its mix within 8 tries. A hint is available for each breed slot, and finishing reveals a link to adopt the dog you just played.
- [Nerdcube - Carom](https://www.nerdcube.games/carom?ref=assets.dles.gg) - Slide an orange target piece around an 8x8 board to reach the goal square in as few moves as possible. Pieces keep sliding until a wall or another piece stops them, so blue blockers have to be manoeuvred into place first to create the stopping points you need.
